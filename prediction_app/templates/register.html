Before you fill this in

What we collect
Your name, mobile number, age, height and weight.
Why
So a clinician reviewing your symptom check has context. These details are not used by the symptom checker itself — it works from your symptoms only.
Who sees it
HealthGuard staff. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.
How long we keep it
Until you withdraw consent, or three years without contact from you.
Withdrawing
You can withdraw at any time from the withdraw consent page, and we will delete your record. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent.
