How it works, honestly

  • It suggests, it does not diagnose. You get a ranked list of possibilities with a match score, never a single verdict.
  • It says when it does not know. If your symptoms do not match anything it recognises, it tells you so and points you to a doctor instead of guessing.
  • It will not suggest medicines. Only a registered medical practitioner can prescribe.
  • You control your data. We tell you what we collect before you enter it, and you can withdraw consent at any time.
