Privacy notice

Version 2026-09-20.1

What we collect

When you start a symptom check we ask for your name, mobile number, age, height and weight. When you use the checker we process the symptoms you select and how long you have had them.

Why we collect it

So that a clinician reviewing your symptom check has context about you. Your name, age, height and weight are not used by the symptom checker — it works from your symptoms only.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not recommend, suggest or prescribe medicines.
  • We do not diagnose you.

How long we keep it

Until you withdraw consent, or three years without any contact from you, whichever comes first.

Your choices

Giving your details is voluntary and you can withdraw consent at any time, which deletes your record. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent.

Contact

For questions about your data, or to make a request about it, contact the HealthGuard team. A named grievance contact and response times will be published here before the service is offered to the public.

About the symptom checker

The checker is a machine-learning model trained on a public symptom-disease dataset. It outputs ranked possibilities with a match score, and declines to answer when its confidence is too low. It is a triage aid, not a medical device, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
